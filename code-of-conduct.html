Code of Conduct

Creating a respectful, welcoming, and accountable community.

Our Community Standard

Rising Tide is committed to building a community rooted in respect, dignity, and care. This Code of Conduct outlines the expectations for how we engage with one another, how we show up in shared spaces, and how we maintain a safe and welcoming environment for all.

What This Policy Covers

The full document includes expectations related to respectful communication, inclusion, accountability, and community standards. It is designed to help ensure that our spaces feel welcoming, safe, and aligned with our mission.

  • Respectful communication and interpersonal conduct
  • Inclusion and access for all participants
  • Accountability and conflict resolution
  • Shared standards for gathering and collaboration
