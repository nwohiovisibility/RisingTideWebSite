Welcome

Rising Tide is a growing community effort rooted in visibility, belonging, and connection. We are creating spaces where people can feel seen, supported, and invited to participate.

Our work centers on building community, lifting up voices, and helping neighbors connect across difference with care, respect, and shared purpose.

Rising Tide is officially registered as a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization.

Rising Tide is the parent organization of the Northwest Ohio Visibility Alliance (NOVA), a regional initiative focused on community visibility, belonging, and empowerment.

What We’re Building

We are gathering people who care about stronger community ties, broader visibility, and a more welcoming Northwest Ohio. Whether you are looking to connect, volunteer, learn more, or simply be part of a growing effort, there is a place for you here.

Why It Matters

When people feel visible and valued, communities grow stronger. Rising Tide is centered on the belief that every person deserves to be seen, supported, and included in the life of the community.
