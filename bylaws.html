Bylaws

Guiding principles for how Rising Tide operates and serves community.

Purpose

Rising Tide is organized to support community visibility, connection, and mutual care. These bylaws help define the organization’s purpose, structure, and approach to serving the community in a transparent and accountable way.

What This Document Covers

The official bylaws provide the formal framework for decision-making, governance, and the organization's guiding commitments. They outline the purpose, responsibilities, and general operating principles that help keep the work grounded and consistent.

  • Organizational purpose and community focus
  • Leadership and accountability principles
  • Operational guidance for the group
  • Shared standards for decision-making and collaboration

Official Record

The full official version of the bylaws is preserved as a PDF for formal reference. This page provides the context and public-facing summary while the downloadable version remains the official record.
