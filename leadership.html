Our Leadership

Rising Tide is guided by leaders committed to visibility, belonging, and community empowerment. This page introduces the people who help shape our mission and support our work across Northwest Ohio.

Executive Board

  • After serving in the US Navy, Brian Von Wert settled in Northwest Ohio, where he spent more than twenty years working for a large corporation and building deep ties in the community. He eventually shifted into local activism and has served as chair of the Fulton County Ohio Democratic Party for three and a half years. He brings that experience to his work across the region, focusing on connection, belonging, and making civic spaces feel human.
