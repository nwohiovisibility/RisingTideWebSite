Join Rising Tide

Rising Tide is a growing community effort rooted in visibility, belonging, and care. We welcome people who want to contribute, connect, learn, and help build something meaningful for Northwest Ohio. No prior experience is required—just a willingness to participate and show up with respect and community-minded energy.

Privacy and use of information: The information you share here is used to stay connected with the community, coordinate opportunities, and support the work of Rising Tide and NOVA. It will be kept confidential and used only for the purpose of community-building and outreach.
